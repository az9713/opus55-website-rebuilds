Architect's statement
“Our aim was a building calmer than the view it stands in. Halden frames the harbour rather than rivalling it: a single column of glass, wrapped in bronze fins that hold the light the way water does, stepping back just twice so it reads as one clean line from across the bay.”
Alix TorranceFounding Partner, Torrance Vale Architects

Neighbourhood · Marlin Quay

A working dock, made into a place to live.

For a hundred years, Marlin Quay was a rope works and a ferry yard. The old warehouses hold restaurants and galleries now, the rail sidings became a promenade, and the ferry still runs every twelve minutes.
