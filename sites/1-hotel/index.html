ITHE ORIGIN

In 1962 Nerea Calvesi bought a lemon terrace nobody wanted, on a cliff everyone called too steep. She built four rooms, a kitchen and a staircase down to the water. Her grandchildren run the house now: thirty-four rooms, the same hundred and twelve steps, and a kitchen that still shuts on the nights the boats come home empty.
