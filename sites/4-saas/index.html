06Security & compliance

Ready for the audit you
haven't booked yet.

An agent with access to your books should meet a higher bar than the people it supports. It does.

Read-only by default

Kestrel connects to banks using read-only scopes. Moving money needs a separate grant, approved by two named humans, every time.

Locked down in transit and at rest

TLS 1.3 across the board, AES-256-GCM encryption at rest, and per-tenant keys inside an HSM-backed KMS. Enterprise customers can supply their own.

Nothing here trains a model

Inference runs inside isolated environments. Your ledger, prompts and documents never train any model, ours or anyone else's.

Every action stays on record

Every entry the agent proposes, and every human decision on it, lands in a hash-chained log your auditors can export with one click.
