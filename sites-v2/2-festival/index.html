03THE EXPERIENCE

24 HOURS
IN THEjungle

A loose timeline of a Sinkglow Saturday. Times are approximate. Sunburn not included, but probable.

06:40

The campsite stirs

Mist between the hammocks, someone's coffee pot hissing on a gas burner, a stranger offering you half a sweet roll. Camp Ceiba at dawn is the quietest eighteen thousand people ever get.

Coffee brewing 6am
10:15

The cenote dip

The Blue Eye below the Bluewell stage turns into the world's clearest hangover cure. Lifeguards on duty from 9am. Screaming allowed, expected, basically required.

On duty 9–5
19:52

Golden hour at the Big Ceiba

The sun sinks behind the main stage, the confetti cannons fire, and you hug a stranger from the refill queue. This becomes your profile picture for the next eleven months.

Confetti is paper. Naturally.
21:10

The art wander

The Jade Toucan glows in the clearing: ten metres of woven rattan and glass lantern, built by a dozen people over nine weeks. Walk beneath it, lose your friends, find new ones.

14 art works
01:10

Lanternfall after dark

Lasers cutting through jungle mist, a kick drum you feel in your molars, and the howler monkeys answering every drop from the trees. Bedtime is a rumour.

    09GETTING THERE

    ALMENDRA TO THE
    EDGE OF THE MAP

    Ixkanil Sinks sits 2 hours 10 from Puerto Almendra and about 40 years from the nearest decent phone signal. Plan accordingly.

    PUERTO ALMENDRACRUCE CHACÁPUNTA CAREYIXKANIL SINKS
    A

    The Sinkglow Shuttle

    Every 20 minutes from Puerto Almendra, Friday 9am to Monday 2pm. Two hours ten with a stop at Punta Carey for tamales.

    $59 RETURNADDED AT CHECKOUT
    B

    Drive

    Verdeluz Highway the whole way. Three or more in the car and you park in Lot A by the gate. One or two and you walk from Lot D. Them's the rules.

    $45 CAR PASSPER CAR, NOT PER HEAD
    C

    Ride

    84 km from Almendra, flat as a tortilla except the climb at Cruce Chacá. Free bike valet at Gate B, and a cold coconut on us if you arrive sweaty.

    FREE VALETHEROES ONLY
