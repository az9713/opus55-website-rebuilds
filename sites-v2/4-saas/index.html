02Inside the agent's process

Every count lands
with its reasoning.

Wardlight documents its work the way a senior pharmacist would explain it to you. Trace one cabinet removal from the drawer to the record.

01
Capture

A nurse removes 2 mg of hydromorphone from cabinet 4W-A12 at 02:14. Wardlight picks it up inside a minute.

02
Match

It finds order ORD-88213 for bed 4W-17: same drug, 1 mg dose, due at 02:00, verified by a pharmacist.

03
Account

1 mg given at the bedside, 1 mg wasted with a witness, logged the way your team logged the previous eleven.

04
Explain

It writes a plain-English explanation with the evidence behind it. Below 95% confidence, it asks a pharmacist instead.

05
Record

CS-10488 closes with a hash-chained audit record. Your pharmacist reviews it in the morning, not at 2am.

Read-only by default

Wardlight connects to cabinets and the EHR using read-only scopes. Unlocking a drawer needs a separate grant, approved by two licensed humans, every time.

TLS 1.3 across the board, AES-256-GCM encryption at rest, and per-tenant keys inside an HSM-backed KMS. Health systems can supply their own.
