Architect's statement
“Our aim was a building that gives back more green than it takes. Arvora stands among ten thousand towers and does not compete with them: a single column of raw concrete, its balconies deep enough to hold real trees, stepping back just twice so the gardens climb it like a hillside.”
Lívia OkamotoFounding Partner, Bastos Okamoto Arquitetos
01
One move

Two setbacks, at Levels 12 and 24, and nothing more. Seen from the avenue, the tower reads as one clean line.

02
Plain materials

Concrete cast against pine boards, hardwood inside, clay underfoot. The concrete stays raw, so it darkens with each summer storm, exactly as planned.

03
Shade above all

Floor-to-ceiling glass, with 3.1-metre ceilings. Deep planted balconies cut summer heat gain by 38%, without tinting a single pane.

Neighbourhood · Alto das Paineiras

A mill district, made into a place to live.

For eighty years, Alto das Paineiras was textile mills and rail yards. The old mill sheds hold restaurants and galleries now, the freight line became a raised garden, and the metro stops every three minutes.
